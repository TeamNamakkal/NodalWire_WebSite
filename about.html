Our Founding

A firm built on one conviction — and 24 years of proof

NodalWire LLC was founded in 2025 and registered in Delaware. We are a US-based network engineering firm delivering fixed-scope design, deployment, and maintenance engagements across microwave, optical, FTTH, Wi-Fi, and access networks, serving clients globally.

The firm was built by an engineer with 24 years of hands-on experience across carrier networks in the United States, India, Nigeria, and beyond. NodalWire was founded on one conviction: that clients deserve a partner who treats their network as if they own its outcome and stays accountable for how it performs long after the engagement closes.

We do not sell equipment. We do not take vendor commissions. Every recommendation is driven purely by what the network requires.

Our primary work spans fixed-scope network engineering projects and hands-on technical training — keeping engagements focused, transparent, and outcome-driven.

Wherever we work, we give high priority to working with engineers from the country we serve, because the best networks are built by people who understand the environment they operate in.

Purpose

Where we're going, and how we get there

Vision

To transform network operations into a predictable, self-healing function.

Infrastructure that anticipates failure, scales on demand, and runs with minimal manual intervention. NodalWire will be the embedded engineering partner that organizations trust to manage their most critical systems — the way an owner would.

Mission

We take complete ownership of our clients' networks.

Designing them to scale without disruption, operating them to prevent outages before they occur, and continuously optimizing them as demand evolves. We combine engineering depth with AI-driven automation to identify and resolve critical infrastructure challenges upstream — reducing operational friction and enabling our partners to focus on their business, not their network.

Core Values

The standards we hold ourselves to
